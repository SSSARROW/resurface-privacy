Norlo

Save it. It resurfaces when it matters.

Norlo is an Android app for saving screenshots, links, and quick notes, then bringing them back later as a plain-language digest, at a moment that's actually useful instead of buried and forgotten.

What Norlo does

Screenshot a recipe mid-scroll. Share a reel you'll "watch properly later." Jot a thought before it slips. Norlo catches all of it, then does the part most save-it-for-later tools skip: reading it, summarizing it, and reminding you when it's actually relevant.

Share-sheet capture

Share any link, reel, or screenshot into Norlo from any app.

Floating quick-capture bubble

Tap for a note, hold to also save a screenshot, without switching apps.

Screenshot prompt

Take a screenshot the normal way and get a one-tap save-or-skip prompt.

AI digest

Every save gets a plain-language summary, key points, and a verdict on whether it's worth your time.

Timed resurfacing

A push notification brings saved items back at a moment chosen to be useful, not a nag.

Automatic cleanup

Screenshots with no note attached clean themselves up automatically after a period you control.

Sign-in and data

Accounts are secured with Supabase Auth (email/password or Google Sign-In). Content you save is processed to build your digest; the full detail on what's collected, why, and who it's shared with is in the privacy policy linked below. No ads, no data sold, no analytics SDK. Delete any single item, or your whole account, from inside the app at any time.
