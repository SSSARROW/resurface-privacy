Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your Norlo account and everything saved in it, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Published by SSARROW (personal developer account)
•

Deleting your account is immediate and permanent: every capture, every image, your push registration, and your sign-in identity are all removed. There is no recovery window and no soft-delete.

If you still have Norlo installed

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Norlo and sign in if you aren't already.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top right to open Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

That's it - deletion happens immediately, with no additional waiting period.

If you've already uninstalled Norlo

Request deletion by email

Email sahansamadhi143@gmail.com from the address associated with your Norlo account, with the subject line "Delete my Norlo account." Include the email you signed up with if it's different from the one you're writing from.

Your account and all associated data will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a confirmation once it's done.

Deleting individual items instead

You don't have to delete your whole account

To remove a single screenshot, link, or note without touching the rest of your account: open that item in Norlo and tap the trash icon at the top of the screen, then confirm. This deletes just that item immediately - your account and everything else stay untouched.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identity (email/password or Google-linked identity)
  • Every capture you saved: screenshots, links, and notes
  • All digests, summaries, and verdicts generated from your captures
  • Your push-notification registration

For more detail on what's collected and why in the first place, see the Privacy Policy.
