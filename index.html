01

Overview

Norlo is a save-things app: screenshots, links, and quick notes go in, and a plain-language summary comes back out later, at a time chosen to actually be useful instead of buried and forgotten.

This policy covers the Norlo Android app and the backend service it talks to. It's written in plain language on purpose: if something here is unclear, the contact section at the bottom is a real inbox, not a formality.

•

The short version: your saves get processed to build a summary, that processing involves a small number of named third-party services, nothing is sold or shown ads against, and you can delete any of it, including your whole account, from inside the app at any time.

02

What we collect

Account information

Signing in uses Supabase Auth, a third-party identity provider. Creating an account collects your email address and a securely hashed password (or, if you use "Continue with Google," the account info Google provides for sign-in). We never see or store your password in readable form; Supabase handles that.

Content you save

Whatever you actually save: screenshots and photos you capture or attach, links you paste, and any note you type or dictate. If you use voice input, speech-to-text conversion happens through your device's own speech recognition; the audio itself isn't sent to Norlo's servers, only the resulting text.

Automatically collected

A push-notification token (so we can send you a resurfacing reminder or a weekly recap) and a device identifier used solely to route pushes to the right device, not for tracking, profiling, or advertising.

03

How we use it

  • Digesting. Screenshots, links, and notes are sent to a third-party AI provider to generate the summary, key points, and verdict you see for that item.
  • Enrichment. When relevant, a short search query (not your raw content) is sent to YouTube, TMDB, or Brave Search to surface a real related video, streaming link, or article.
  • Resurfacing. Content and your reactions to it (Done / Remind me later / Dead) determine when we send a push reminder or include something in your weekly recap.
  • Keeping the app working. Account identifiers and device tokens are used to authenticate requests and deliver push notifications, nothing more.
•

We don't run ads, we don't have an ad SDK in the app, and we don't sell or rent your data to anyone, for any reason.

05

How long we keep it

Retention is different for a saved screenshot's image versus its summary, on purpose.

Screenshots without a note

If you don't attach a note to a screenshot, the image itself is automatically deleted after a period you control (45 days by default, adjustable in Settings). You get two heads-up notifications before it happens, and a review screen where you can keep it, which resets the clock rather than keeping it forever, since the only thing that keeps something permanently is attaching a note to it.

Everything else

A digest's summary, verdict, and key points are kept until you delete that item yourself. Anything with a note attached to it is never auto-deleted.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account in-app is immediate and permanent: every capture, every image, your push registration, and your Supabase identity itself are all removed. There's no recovery window and no soft-delete. Once it's done, it's done.

06

Your controls

  • Settings Adjust how long no-note screenshots are kept before automatic deletion.
  • Review screen See everything about to be cleaned up and rescue individual items.
  • Any item Delete a single capture, or add a note to keep its image indefinitely.
  • Account Delete your entire account and all associated data from inside the app, anytime.

None of these require contacting us first; they're built into the app itself.

07

Security

All traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Every request is authenticated, access to your data is scoped strictly to your own account, and the backend is hardened against common abuse patterns: rate limits on account creation, validated uploads, and no third-party ad or analytics trackers embedded in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we treat your saved content as something worth actually protecting, not an afterthought.

08

Children's privacy

Norlo isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

09

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that meaningfully affects how your data is handled, we'll update the "last updated" date above and, where practical, note it in the app. Continuing to use Norlo after a change means you accept the updated policy.
